SKIP PENALTY IN CHALLENGES (v1.8.2)

Copy these files over your naija-sabi folder (replace the old ones):
  src/routes/challenges.js  public/js/app.js
  scripts/smoke-test.js  scripts/group-test.js
  package.json  README.md  docs/CHANGELOG.md

Then run: npm test and npm start. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.
Skipping a puzzle in a challenge now costs 10 points. Highest score still wins; ties go to the faster player.
