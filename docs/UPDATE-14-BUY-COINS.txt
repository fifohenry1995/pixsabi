BUY COINS WITH PAYSTACK (v1.9.0)

Copy these files over your naija-sabi folder (replace the old ones, add the new ones):
  NEW: src/paystack.js  src/routes/coins.js  scripts/pay-test.js  scripts/orders.js
  src/db.js  src/app.js
  public/js/app.js  public/js/game.js  public/css/style.css  public/sw.js
  package.json  README.md  docs/CHANGELOG.md

Then run: npm test and npm start. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

Turn it on (see "Buy coins with Paystack" in README.md):
  PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY = your Paystack secret key (sk_test_... first)
  PUBLIC_URL = your https website address
  and put https://YOUR-SITE/api/paystack/webhook as the Webhook URL in the Paystack dashboard.
Without the key the Buy button says "not open yet" and nothing is charged.
See purchases with: npm run orders
Prices and packs: PACKS at the top of src/routes/coins.js
