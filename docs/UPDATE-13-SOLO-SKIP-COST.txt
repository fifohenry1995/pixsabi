SOLO SKIP COSTS COINS (v1.8.3)

Copy these files over your naija-sabi folder (replace the old ones):
  src/routes/solo.js
  public/js/app.js  public/js/game.js
  scripts/smoke-test.js
  package.json  README.md  docs/CHANGELOG.md

Then run: npm test and npm start. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.
Solo Skip now costs 20 coins (players start with 100 and earn 10 per solved puzzle).
To change the price, edit SKIP_COST in src/routes/solo.js and skipCost in public/js/app.js.
