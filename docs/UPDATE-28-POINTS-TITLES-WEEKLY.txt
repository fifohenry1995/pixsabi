UPDATE 28 (v2.9.0): giving points a purpose

1. RANK TITLES (from total points). Newcomer 0, Learner 300, Sabi Person 1,000, Gist Master 2,500, Oga Sabi 5,000, Area Champion 8,000, Naija Legend 12,000.
   Shown on the Me page (with a progress bar to the next title), on profiles, in chat (a small icon next to the name) and on the leaderboards.
   Edit the numbers or add titles in src/titles.js (add higher ones as you add more puzzles).
2. WEEKLY LEADERBOARD WITH COIN PRIZES. The week runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 Lagos time. When it ends the top 3 get coins: 1st 200, 2nd 100, 3rd 50
   (players need at least 100 points that week to qualify). The winners get an alert and the coins at once, and the first place gets the "Weekly champion" badge.
   The week is paid exactly once (recorded in the database), also after a restart. If the server was off for more than a week, only the latest finished week can be paid.
   Change prizes and the minimum in src/weekly.js (PRIZES, MIN_POINTS).
3. PER-CATEGORY LEADERBOARDS. The Ranks page now has four tabs: Overall, This week, Naija, Universal. Points are counted per category (from solo, the daily puzzle and races).
   Players from before this update get their Naija and Universal totals worked out automatically the first time the new version starts.
4. PHOTO FRAMES AND BADGES. The frame around a player's photo changes colour with their title (bronze, silver, gold, glowing gold, green glow, and the green-white-green Legend frame).
   Badges on the Me page (locked ones are greyed with how to earn them) and on other players' profiles (earned only):
   First race win, 10 race wins, 7-day streak, 30-day streak, Naija 25, Naija 100, Universal 10, Universal 50, Weekly champion. Edit in src/titles.js.

Still true: points cannot be spent and never go down, so rankings stay about skill. Coins are separate.
Technical: new files src/points.js (adds points everywhere), src/titles.js, src/weekly.js; users table gets 4 new columns (added automatically on start); scripts/ranks-test.js (8 checks).
Anti-cheat note: weekly prizes are small and need 100 points, but several accounts owned by one person can still each earn them. Watch the weekly winners for repeat names.
