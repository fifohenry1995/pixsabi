ZOOM THE PUZZLE PICTURE (v1.9.1)

Copy these files over your naija-sabi folder (replace the old ones):
  public/js/game.js  public/css/style.css
  package.json  README.md  docs/CHANGELOG.md

Then press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser (phones: close and reopen the page). No restart needed for these files.
Tap the puzzle picture to zoom: pinch, double-tap, wheel or + / - buttons. Drag to move. Tap outside, press Esc or tap X to close.
