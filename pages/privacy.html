Who can play

PixSabi is only for people aged 18 and above. You confirm this when you create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a player is under 18, we will close the account and delete the information.

Adverts

Adverts shown in the game are pictures, GIFs or videos that we choose and upload ourselves.{{ADS_SOURCE}} If you tap an advert, you may go to the advertiser's website, which has its own privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

We count, without your name, how many times each advert is shown, tapped or closed, using a random number stored on your device. We also keep daily totals of how many people play and what they play. These totals may be shown to our advertisers and investors. They never include names, emails or other details that identify you.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the companies that help us run the game, and only what they need:

  • Paystack to take payments for coins.
  • Our email service to send password reset and account emails.
  • Our hosting provider to store the game and its data.
  • Browser and phone notification services (such as those run by Google, Apple, Mozilla and Microsoft) if you switch on alerts.

Some of these companies may process information outside Nigeria. We only use providers that protect information properly. We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect the game and its players from fraud and abuse.

Other players can see your username, photo, description, points, rank, title, badges, online status, race results and chat messages. They cannot see your email address, password or coins.

How long we keep information

  • Account and game information: for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, it is deleted 3 days later (see Delete your account).
  • Chat messages: until they are among the oldest and are removed, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Alerts in your inbox: the newest 30.
  • Password reset links: they stop working after 1 hour or after one use.
  • Payment records: for as long as the law or our accounting requires. When an account is deleted we remove the email and name from them.

Keeping your information safe

Passwords are stored in scrambled form. The game is served over a secure connection. We limit who can access the data and we limit repeated login attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password that you do not use anywhere else, and never share it. We will never ask you for your password.

You can also withdraw your consent at any time (for example by switching off alerts, or by asking us to close your account). To do any of these, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account. We will reply within a reasonable time, and within 30 days where the law sets that limit.
