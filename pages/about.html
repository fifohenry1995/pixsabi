About PixSabi

A picture-puzzle word game from Nigeria, for everybody.

You think say you sabi? PixSabi is a game where you look at a picture, think about what it shows, and type the answer. It started with one simple idea: Nigerian food, places, music, stars, slang and everyday life deserve a proper game of their own.

What you can do here

  • Play solo. Climb through the levels at your own speed. Stuck? A letter or a clue costs a few coins.
  • Solve the Daily puzzle. One new puzzle for everyone every day. Build a streak.
  • Race your friends. Pick the number of levels, invite 2 to 8 players, and the first to finish them all wins. Winners are announced in the public chat. Races are free and just for fun.
  • Chat with other players and see who is online.
  • Climb the ranks. Earn points, unlock titles and badges, and fight for the weekly top 3.

Two categories

Naija is all about Nigeria: our food, our places, our stars, our slang and our culture. Universal is for everyone in every country, with puzzles anyone in the world can enjoy. Switch between them any time. Each has its own levels.

Our promise

  • The game is for players aged 18 and above.
  • Puzzle answers stay on our server, so nobody can read them from their phone.
  • Chat has no links, to keep spam and scams out.
  • Coins are only for playing. They are never paid out as cash. Read the Disclaimer and the Privacy Policy for the details.
